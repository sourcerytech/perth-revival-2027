The commons

One night.
Generative work.

The revival is one night. The offering continues as practical tools, shared resources and local acts of repair.

01 / Already in use

The launch succeeds
by disappearing.

Temple, Sourcery and Claimipedia are not meant to become another centre of allegiance. The hope is that their patterns become ordinary: clear sources, honest uncertainty, shared responsibility and useful cooperation.

If the tools work, people may use them without needing to remember who made them.

02 / What the commons makes

Make good work
easy to share.

Churches and communities can adapt open resources for worship, social posts, small groups, youth work, service projects, invitations, translations and follow-up.

Careservice ideas, support directories and volunteer pathways

03 / The handover

Open tools.
Shared stewardship.

Nothing here belongs to one founder, church or platform. The work should be localisable, inspectable, correctable and easy to leave.
