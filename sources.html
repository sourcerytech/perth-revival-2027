01 / The process

A way of asking
what changes.

The Claimipedia process-algebra work asks a careful sequence of questions: what can share a space, what carries the relationship, what remains faithful as things move, what chooses a direction, and what can be recognised afterwards.

For this gathering, those questions become human ones: Can everyone belong? Can we listen without taking over? Can leaders step back? Can prayer become care? Can the night leave behind repair that is visible in ordinary life?

02 / The boundary

A guardrail for
humility.

The framework is not being offered as proof of God, a guarantee of healing, or a mechanism that controls people. It is a discipline for keeping power answerable to love.

That is why this event makes room for doubt, protects consent, refuses to blame people for suffering, and looks for fruit rather than spectacle.
