01 / The invitation

Revival as
shared agency.

The Emptying Flame is a night of prayer, worship, lament, reconciliation and practical mercy beside the Derbarl Yerrigan / Swan River.

Its kenotic centre is simple: power is most trustworthy when it serves rather than dominates. We gather to ask for renewal, while refusing to turn faith into pressure, suffering into blame, or emotion into evidence.

“The power of revival is not how many people fall down, but how many burdens are lifted when we stand.”

04 / Full process algebra

Every claim
has a boundary.

The event translates the corpus’s process discipline into public practice. Nothing gets promoted from symbol to fact without a declared carrier, selector, detector and reopening rule.

CARRIER

Perth in place

The river, skyline, people, histories, churches, services and neighbourhoods that hold the gathering.

SELECTOR

Kenotic invitation

A choice to privilege non-domination, humility, consent, protection and service over control.

DETECTOR

Fruit over spectacle

Look for repaired relationships, safer communities, sustained service and truthful testimony.

BOUNDARY

What we do not claim

The night does not prove God, guarantee healing, explain suffering or make every outcome a confirmation.
