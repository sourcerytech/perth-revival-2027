01 / The invitation

Two stories.
One offering.

Perth Encounter is a night of prayer, worship, lament, reconciliation and practical mercy beside the Derbarl Yerrigan / Swan River.

It grew from one person’s search for God, truth, love and responsible power. It is now offered beyond one person: to Perth’s churches, communities, musicians and neighbours as one night given to God.

Its kenotic centre is simple: power is most trustworthy when it serves rather than dominates. We take revelation seriously without turning testimony into control, and we make room for doubt, conscience and honest disagreement.

“The power of revival is not how many people fall down, but how many burdens are lifted when we stand.”

04 / A way of living

What we
release,
what we receive.

Kenosis is not disappearing. It is choosing not to take up all the room. We loosen our grip, make space for another person, and let love become something that can be recognised in the way we live.

01 · BEGIN

Make a welcome

There is room for the hopeful, the doubtful, the grieving and the tired. No one has to perform belief to belong.

02 · STAY PRESENT

Receive this place

We honour Perth, its waters, its peoples and its stories. We arrive as guests, not owners.

03 · PROTECT

Keep one another safe

Love does not consume another person’s freedom. Consent, safeguarding, dignity and honest boundaries matter.

04 · LISTEN

Let love move

Prayer, music, silence and testimony become a way of listening—without rushing to fix, explain or control.

05 · RELEASE

Give up the centre

At 7:07, leaders step back. We release the need to impress, dominate or produce a spectacle.

06 · RETURN

Look for the fruit

We return to ordinary life carrying repair: a safer home, a shared meal, a hard apology, a neighbour remembered.

welcome→listen→release→receive→repair→return
