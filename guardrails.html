01 / The answer

The accuser says X.
We answer Y.

“You are separating from society.” We answer: we build operational independence while remaining relationally open.

“You want power.” We answer: power is given away through service, oversight, dissent and easy exit.

“You claim revelation, so people must obey.” We answer: testimony can be honoured while conscience remains free.

02 / Humane participation

More dignity.
More freedom.

Consentno forced prayer, disclosure, conversion or emotional response
Safetychild protection, safeguarding, accessibility and quiet spaces
Accountabilityplural oversight, clear complaints and external relationships
Exitno penalty for disagreement, departure or changing one’s mind
Truthno staged miracles, prosperity promises or blame for suffering
03 / Open finances

The treasury
is a servant.

Income, expenditure, donor categories, conflicts of interest, compensation and surplus allocations should be understandable and publicly reported.

Any future cryptocurrency is a separate experiment in shared infrastructure—not a spiritual badge, promise of profit or requirement for belonging. Legal, tax and security review comes before launch.
